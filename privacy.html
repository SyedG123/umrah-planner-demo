Umrah Planner — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 July 2026 · Applies to the Umrah Planner Android app and web demo · Contact: isyedreyann@gmail.com

Umrah Planner is a trip-planning and agent-comparison app for pilgrims, operated from Bengaluru, India. It is built to work without an account: your plans, profile and saved quotes live on your device. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, when, and why.

What stays on your device

Your pilgrim profile (name, phone number), saved plans, quotes you enter for comparison, checklist progress and app settings are stored only in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot read them.

What leaves your device, and when

DataWhenWhere it goesWhy
Your name, phone number and trip request Only when you tap to send a quote request 1) The travel agent you chose, via WhatsApp (you see and send the message yourself); 2) a copy to our lead log (a Google Sheet we operate) Delivering your request to the agent; tracking that agents respond properly; billing agents for leads (never you)
A random install identifier and usage events (e.g. "plan created", "quote requested" — never your plans' contents) While you use the app Our operations log (a Google Sheet we operate) Understanding what works during the pilot; no advertising, no sale of data, not linked to your name unless you send a quote request
Route and travel dates (no identity) When live flight prices are shown or checked Our fare file on GitHub's CDN; exact-date checks go through our Cloudflare proxy to SerpAPI (Google Flights results) Showing you real market fares instead of guesses
Quote figures and your own plan's cost lines (no name, no phone) Only if you tap the optional AI comparison Our Cloudflare proxy → Z.AI (the AI model provider) Generating the quote-comparison report; the app labels this AI-generated guidance

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No account is created in this version of the app, and no payment is collected from pilgrims — the app is free for pilgrims.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos or files are accessed.

Security and retention

Everything sent off-device travels over HTTPS. Lead and usage records are kept for the duration of the pilot programme and routine business records thereafter, then deleted or anonymised.

Deleting your data

Email isyedreyann@gmail.com with the name and phone number you used in a quote request and we will delete your lead and usage records within 30 days. Data already shared with an agent via your own WhatsApp message is between you and that agent — we recommend contacting them directly as well.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning pilgrimage travel and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change what data is handled (for example, optional sign-in for verified reviews), this policy will be updated here first and the effective date revised.
